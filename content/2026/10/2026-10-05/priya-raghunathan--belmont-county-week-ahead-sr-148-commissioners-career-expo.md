**Two state road work zones are still active in Belmont County this week, one of them until the end of
November.** The Board of Commissioners meets Wednesday morning, a career expo for area teens runs the same
morning, township trustees meet Thursday night, and Saturday is the busiest day on the county calendar.

### Getting around

- **State Route 148, bridge over North Fork Captina Creek.** Work began Sept. 28 to repair the bridge
  carrying SR 148 over the creek, about three miles east of SR 800. SR 148 is reduced to one lane with
  temporary traffic signals. Estimated completion: **Nov. 30, 2026.**
- **State Route 147, slide repair west of Key.** ODOT is stabilizing two slopes along SR 147 west of Key.
  The work zone runs from Wegee Road to Tiber Road, with temporary traffic signals maintaining traffic.
  Estimated completion: **Oct. 31, 2026.**
- **Four other projects in ODOT's Sept. 26 weekly update were dated to finish before this week:** the
  SR 149 slide-repair closure west of Warnock (detour via SR 9 south to SR 147 west, then SR 147 west back
  to SR 149), the SR 149 bridge rehabilitation over Little McMahon Creek, U.S. 40 resurfacing on 11 miles
  from Ladybend Hill (at I-70, mile marker 204) east to the western end of St. Clairsville, and the SR 7
  southbound lane reduction just south of the Picoma Road exit (Sept. 28 – Oct. 1). Those dates have passed.
  **I could not confirm from ODOT itself whether they finished on schedule — see source notes. Check OHGO
  before leaving the house.**

### Also under construction, not a road work zone this week

- **I-70 at Exit 208 (SR 149/Morristown), interchange improvement.** This is a separate, long-running
  project and is not one of the two state road work zones above. ODOT lists begin construction fall 2025,
  end construction summer 2029, and an estimated construction cost of **$25,340,000**, work category
  "Interchange Improvement (Safety)," District 11, Belmont County. Two lanes are currently maintained on
  I-70 in both directions. Traffic impacts to SR 149 itself are not expected until 2027.

### This week's calendar

- **Today, Mon. Oct. 5** — Two Ohio Valley Mall promotions begin today and run through Oct. 26: "Monster
  Maul Text of Treat" SMS promotion, Center Court; "Blue the Pumpkin Storybook Contest," Center Court.
- **Tue., Oct. 6 — Village of Bellaire committee meetings**, all in council chambers on the second floor of
  the municipal building: Street, Building and Property Committee at **9:30 a.m.**; working meeting with the
  fiscal officer and council at **10 a.m.**; Health and Safety Committee at **noon**.
- **Wed., Oct. 7, 10 a.m. — Belmont County Board of Commissioners** regular meeting, Belmont County
  Courthouse, 101 West Main Street, St. Clairsville. The county publishes a standing schedule of
  Wednesdays at 10 a.m. for 2026, held until adjournment. No advance agenda is posted; the most recent
  signed minutes on the county's site are from Sept. 16, 2026.
- **Wed., Oct. 7, 9 a.m. to noon — Belmont–Harrison Career & Transition Expo**, ECO Center, 68332
  Bannock Road, St. Clairsville. More than 500 students from high schools and career centers across
  Belmont, Harrison and Noble counties are expected; more than 50 agencies, businesses and colleges will
  be present. The ECO Center is a fully accessible venue. Sponsored by the Belmont-Harrison Multi-Agency
  Planning Group. Third annual event.
- **Thu., Oct. 8 — Annual Belmont County Township Trustee meeting**, Wise Building, Belmont County
  Fairgrounds. Doors open **5 p.m.**, meeting begins **6 p.m.** Dinner will be served.
- **Sat.–Sun., Oct. 10–11** — Pumpkin Smash Softball Tournament.
- **Sat. Oct. 10, 8 a.m. to Sun. Oct. 11, 5 p.m. — AKC Dog Show**, Belmont County Fairgrounds, 45420
  Roscoe Road, St. Clairsville, with obedience and rally trials.
- **Sat., Oct. 10, 6–9 p.m.** — Live music at Vino di Piccin Tasting House.
- **Sat., Oct. 10 — Ohio Valley Mall:** "Cutest Pet Contest: Halloween Edition with Belmont County Animal
  Shelter," Boscov's Court; "Magic and Malarkey: A Family Magic Show by The Alans," Center Court.

### Look ahead

- **Mon., Oct. 19, 6 p.m.** — Belmont-Harrison Vocational School District Board of Education regular October
  meeting, Belmont Career Center, 68090 Hammond Road, St. Clairsville.
- **Fri., Nov. 13, 2 p.m. — ribbon-cutting and public opening of the Underground Railroad Experience at
  the Black Horse Inn**, 203 E. Cross Street, Morristown. Belmont County Tourism Council Executive Director
  Jackee Pugh announced the date to the Board of Commissioners at its Wednesday Sept. 16, 2026 meeting,
  and it is recorded in the county's signed minutes for that date. The museum was founded in 1993 by the late Dr. John Mattox and
  his late wife, Rosalind Mattox, and has been at 121 E. High Street in Flushing; the 1807 inn is on the Old National Road. The
  move was funded by $3.9 million from the state Appalachian Community Grant Program awarded in 2024.
