BELMONT COUNTY — The Belmont County Board of Commissioners has posted minutes for 36 of the 39 Wednesdays that fell between January 7 and September 30, 2026, and the county's public index of those minutes stops on September 16.

The index at belmontcountycommissioners.com/2026-minutes lists 40 sets of minutes for 2026. The oldest is dated January 7. The newest is dated September 16. There is no entry for the September 23 regular session and no entry for the September 30 regular session.

Both dates fall on a Wednesday, the day the county's own website says the board meets. The home page states: "The Belmont County Commissioner meetings for the year 2026 will be held on Wednesday of each week from 10:00am until adjournment at their office located in the Belmont County Courthouse, 101 West Main Street, St. Clairsville, OH 43950." That makes Wednesday, October 7 the board's next scheduled regular session — two days after this article was published.

## Not the first gap in 2026

The 40 entries are not 40 Wednesdays. Thirty-six are dated on a Wednesday. The other four are meetings held on other days: a reorganization meeting on Monday, January 12; an emergency meeting on Monday, April 6; a special meeting on Monday, May 11; and a regular session on Thursday, May 14.

Counting only Wednesdays, 37 fell between January 7 and September 16. Thirty-six have posted minutes. One does not: Wednesday, May 13. The index does show a regular session the following day, Thursday, May 14. Those minutes record Commissioner Jerry Echemann as absent. Page 1 reads: "Present: Vince Gianangeli and J. P. Dutton, Commissioners, Jessica Koller, Assistant Clerk and Bonnie Zuzak, Clerk of the Board. Absent: Commissioner Jerry Echemann." Every roll call in those minutes reads "Mr. Echemann  Absent."

Nothing in the minutes the county has posted establishes whether the board also met on May 13 and did not post the minutes, or moved that week's session to May 14, or did not hold one. That is not known.

What the index shows is that no minutes are posted for Wednesday, May 13. The session the county did post the next day, Thursday, May 14, is the reason a reader should not read the May 13 line as a skipped meeting. The September gap is the longest run of missing minutes in the posted 2026 record to date: two consecutive Wednesdays, September 23 and September 30, with nothing posted for either, and a third — this week's — that has not happened yet.

## What the September 23 session was scheduled to include

The missing September 23 minutes matter because the county itself put a meeting into that session.

The county advertised sealed bids for a new Belmont County animal shelter. The advertisement appears in full inside the minutes of the September 9 regular session and again on the county's proposals page. It states: "A Pre-Bid meeting will convene at 101 W Main St., St. Clairsville, OH 43950 on Wednesday, September 23, 2026 at 1:15 p.m. Prospective bidders are encouraged to attend, but it is not mandatory."

The same advertisement set a deadline of 11:15 a.m. on Wednesday, October 14 for physical bids, and required questions and substitution requests to reach the architect no later than 11:00 a.m. on Wednesday, September 30.

The September 16 minutes do not mention a pre-bid meeting. The only animal shelter item in them is a motion to accept the resignation of Thomas Platt, full-time Assistant Dog Warden at the Belmont County Animal Shelter, effective September 18, 2026.

So the session the county scheduled a pre-bid meeting into has no posted record of what happened in it.

## The September 16 record, and what the PDFs are

The September 16 minutes show the three commissioners and Bonnie Zuzak, Clerk of the Board, present. The board approved bills "IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF $934,824.95," took up transfers between funds, entered executive session at 11:11 a.m., left executive session at 12:19 p.m. and adjourned at 1:38 p.m.

Those minutes close with the line "Read, approved and signed this 23rd day of September, 2026." The September 16 meeting was held on the 16th and signed on the 23rd — a one-week gap between the meeting and its approval.

The first page of those minutes carries a stamp. It reads: "MEETINGS ARE NOW BEING RECORDED ... ALL DISCUSSIONS ARE SUMMARIZED. FOR COMPLETE PROCEEDINGS PLEASE SEE CORRESPONDING CD FOR THIS MEETING DAY." That document runs six pages, and the stamp is on page 1 only. Pages 2 through 6 do not carry it.

By the county's own stamp, the posted PDF is a summary. The complete proceedings are recorded on a CD from that meeting day. Readers looking for the full text of a commissioners' meeting will not find it on this site.

## The county's rule for posting minutes

The 2026 minutes page carries the county's posting rule in its introduction: "In our ongoing effort to allow the citizens of Belmont County greater access to the Board of Commissioners, we will be publishing the minutes from the public meetings of the Board. These postings will be made once the minutes are finalized and signed by the Board of Commissioners and the Clerk of the Board of Commissioners."

A posting lag and a missing meeting are not the same thing, and nothing in this article says the board failed to meet on September 23 or September 30. The narrow, checkable fact is that no minutes are posted for those dates and the county's own index ends on September 16.

The county's News & Events page carries no notice about the missing minutes.

## A stale page a reader can land on first

The county site also has a page titled "Meeting Minutes" at belmontcountycommissioners.com/meeting-minutes. It opens normally. Its heading is "2023 Board of County Commissioner Minutes," it lists 52 entries, all from 2023, and its newest is December 20, 2023.

That page is not where the current record is. The current record is the 2026 index linked above. The site's separate archive page, belmontcountycommissioners.com/archived-minutes, lists years 2013 through 2025 and stops at 2025. The home page, the archive page and the stale 2023 page all link to the 2026 index.

## The county has not answered yet

Belmont News asked the Belmont County Commissioners Office on Monday, October 5, 2026 whether minutes exist for the September 23 and September 30 sessions and when they will be posted. That request was not completed before publication. The office can be reached at 740-699-2155, at 101 West Main Street, St. Clairsville. The commissioners are Jerry Echemann, president; Vince Gianangeli, vice president; and J. P. Dutton. The clerk is Bonnie Zuzak.

Until the county answers, whether the September 23 and September 30 minutes exist and when they will appear is unknown. Belmont News will update this article when the county responds.
