The Belmont-Harrison Career & Transition Expo runs from 9 a.m. to noon Wednesday, October 7, at the ECO Center in St. Clairsville. The venue is at 68332 Bannock Road, St. Clairsville, Ohio 43950. The website promoting the event gives a different street address, and it is the only one of the four pages that give the venue an address that does.

Three sources give the Bannock Road address.

The venue's own site says: "Centrally located at 68332 Bannock Road in St. Clairsville, Ohio, The ECO Center offers a convenient and accessible destination for visitors from across the region." It repeats the address with the ZIP code in the footer of every page.

The venue's operator says the same thing. The East Central Ohio Educational Service Center, which acquired the building, told its governing board in a public notice that its regular monthly meeting on June 22, 2026 "will be held at the ECO Center, located at 68332 Bannock Road, St. Clairsville, Ohio 43950."

The Belmont County Board of Developmental Disabilities gives it that address too, in the advance release it issued for this expo: "The ECO Center, located at 68332 Bannock Road, St. Clairsville, is a fully accessible venue."

The page that disagrees is also the page promoting the event. The Belmont-Harrison Roadmap to Transition site, belmontharrisonroadmap.org, carries the expo's date, its hours and its transition ages, and lists the venue on its home page as:

> ECO Center
> 45425 National Rd.
> St. Clairsville, 0H 43950

The state is printed as 0H, a zero followed by the letter H. Of the four pages that give the venue an address, it is the only one that gives a street other than Bannock Road. The expo is in two days, and the Roadmap site says parents are welcome.

The event itself is the third annual Belmont-Harrison Career & Transition Expo, for teens with differing abilities leaving high school. In the Board's release, more than 500 students from high schools and career centers across Belmont, Harrison and Noble counties are expected to attend. That figure is the organizers' projection. It is not a count of students who have registered or who came.

More than 50 agencies, businesses and colleges are set to be on site, the release says, covering careers, college, training programs, transition resources, and resume and interview tips. Sessions listed include virtual-reality job simulations, interactive workshops, and "What Not to Wear" by Goodwill Industries.

The Board and the venue do not describe the building the same way. The release calls it "a fully accessible venue." The venue's own site does not make that claim. It lists a 500-person main floor, bleacher seating for 2,400, a conference room that seats 40, and about 70 on-site parking spaces.

The event is sponsored by the Belmont-Harrison Multi-Agency Planning Group, which the release lists as including the Belmont and Harrison County Boards of Developmental Disabilities, Opportunities for Ohioans with Disabilities, Ohio Employment First, the Belmont County Juvenile Court, Belmont Career Center, Goodwill Industries, Bridgeport Schools, St. Clairsville Schools, Union Local Schools, and the East Central Ohio Educational Service Center.

Both the Board's release and the Roadmap site direct questions to Sean Ragsdale at sean.ragsdale@ood.ohio.gov. The Belmont County Board of Developmental Disabilities is at 68421 Hammond Road, St. Clairsville, Ohio 43950, telephone (740) 695-0407.

The ECO Center was formerly the Health and Physical Education Center at Ohio University Eastern. The service center bought it, and marked the reopening of the renovated building with a ribbon cutting and open house, according to its own news item on the opening, dated August 6, 2026. Its site says the building hosts graduation ceremonies for Ohio University Eastern, Belmont Career Center and St. Clairsville High School. The service center says it serves 20 member districts across seven counties, and that its Belmont County office is at 67400 Betty Lee Wy, St. Clairsville, (740) 695-9773. The Belmont-Harrison Career Centers, a listed partner, are at 68090 Hammond Road, St. Clairsville, (740) 695-9130.

As of 9:50 a.m. Monday, October 5, the Roadmap site was still showing the National Road address. No one involved in the expo is quoted in this story, because no one is on the record about the address.
