Belmont County is taking sealed bids for a new animal shelter, and the deadline is 11:15 a.m. on
Wednesday, October 14.

The project is a single-story, slab-on-grade building of roughly 14,100 square feet at 68401
Hammond Road in St. Clairsville. The advertisement calls it an Omniblock structure.

Commissioner Jerry Echemann said at the board's September 9 meeting that the project is estimated
to be around $6 million.

Bids have to be physical. The commissioners will not accept electronic ones, and they will open
what they receive on the spot at the commissioners' office at 101 West Main Street in St.
Clairsville. Anyone bidding has to attach security worth at least 10 percent of the bid amount,
payable to the Belmont County Commissioners Office.

Contractors pay to get the documents. Bid documents, specifications and drawings are available
from SE Blueprint Co. at 520 South Main Street, Suite 2411 in Akron, at the cost of reproduction
and shipping, with no refunds. The same documents can be looked at without charge during business
hours at TC Architects, 430 Grant Street in Akron, though the office asks bidders to call ahead
because the hours are not fixed.

Questions and substitution requests went to the architect's office at 11 a.m. on September 30 and
that window has closed. The architect's contact of record is Susan Allen. A pre-bid meeting was
scheduled for September 23 and attendance was never mandatory.

The advertisement reserves the right to cancel the solicitation and to accept or reject any bid in
whole or in part.

The commissioners voted on September 9 to advertise for the bids, unanimously. The advertisement
was scheduled to run September 14 and again on September 21.
