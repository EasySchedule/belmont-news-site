## 1. One recommendation

**The Wall That Heals finishes its Belmont County visit this weekend, and the strongest
local story for Saturday's 06:00 edition is a last-call service piece: the wall stands
free and open 24 hours today and tomorrow at the Belmont County Fairgrounds, 45420
Roscoe Road in St. Clairsville, Taps plays at dusk tonight, and the closing ceremony is
at 1:45 p.m. Sunday.**

One note on framing, because it decides whether this story works. The wall arriving, and
Thursday's opening ceremony, are already covered. Three other papers have run those. The
story that is still open on Saturday morning is the **last chance** — hours, address, what
happens tonight, and when it ends. Belmont News should not re-report the arrival. It should
tell a reader who has not gone yet that they can still go tonight.

---

## 2. Why this one

It is the largest live story in the county this weekend, and it is the only one a reader
can still act on before lunch. Every Belmont County reader either drove past the wall on
its escort into St. Clairsville or saw it in the news, and a plain, practical morning
note is more useful to them than another recap of a ceremony they did not attend. The
window is closing: two days left, then it is gone until the next host site. It is also
genuinely a Belmont County story and not a story that happened to pass through — the
county fairgrounds, American Legion St. Clairsville Post 159 as presenting sponsor, the
Belmont County Tourism Council as organizer, and the Belmont County commissioner on the
podium. And it lands on a Saturday, when the people most likely to visit have the day off.

### 3c. Corroboration I am relying on, and its limits

Read this part before you trust the crowd numbers. The opening-ceremony story ran
identically under four mastheads — The Intelligencer, The Times Leader, The Weirton Daily
Times and The Herald Star — all carrying the **same byline, Gage Vota**. That is one
newsroom, not four independent confirmations. I am treating it as a single source.

What carries the load instead is two genuinely separate bodies: the host committee's own
schedule (D1) and the county tourism council's public event listing (D2). Those two are
independent of each other and of Ogden, and they agree on the venue, the address, the dates
and the hours. That is why I cleared the story.

Facts with single-source support, which I have kept out of the recommended sentence:
the Sept 29 escort drawing "more than a thousand" residents, and the size of Thursday's
crowd.

## 5. What you could not verify

Everything in this section I actively checked and could not confirm. None of it is in the
recommended sentence.

1. **Any Belmont County high school football result from Friday, October 2, 2026.** This is
   the important one. ScoreStream displays a line reading "St Clairsville Red Devils defeat
   Bellaire Big Reds 28 to 16" against a game page dated 2026-10-02, but the entry carries
   no byline, no timestamp, and no way to tell which season it describes. OhioPrepFootball
   and USA Prep Football both still render the game as unplayed. Sports On SI shows the
   matchup with no score. I would not publish that number, and I did not use it.
2. **A near-miss worth recording.** A WTRF story titled "Martins Ferry shuts out Buckeye
   Local at home" reads as exactly the confirmation I needed. It is dated 2025-10-03 and
   reports a 45–0 final from the **previous year**. Anyone speed-reading this beat will hit
   that page and get it wrong.
3. **Turnout at Friday night's "Light the Night" ceremony.** The event is on the official
   schedule for 7:45 p.m. on Oct 2. I found no published report of what happened. Do not let
   the morning edition imply a turnout figure.
4. **The "Light the Night" start time is inconsistent across sources.** The official host
   schedule (D1) and the Belmont County Tourism press release both say **7:45 p.m.**
   Lede News, WTRF, and a February Barnesville Area News item all said **7:30 p.m.** Use
   7:45 p.m. Use it in the past tense, or leave it out, since the event is already behind us
   at press time.
5. **The closing ceremony time is inconsistent too.** D1 says 1:45 p.m. and D2 says the
   exhibit closes at 2 p.m. after the ceremony. The September Tourism Council press release,
   as republished by Barnesville Area News, said the closing ceremony is "Sunday, October 4
   at 2 p.m." D1 and The Times Leader and The Intelligencer all say 1:45 p.m. Use **1:45
   p.m.**, and describe the grounds as closing at 2 p.m.
6. **"26 Belmont County Vietnam War service members are featured on The Wall."** Single
   source: the September Tourism Council press release. I could not confirm it anywhere
   else. Do not print it until the committee confirms it.
7. **Hours for the Mobile Education Center specifically.** Both D1 and D2 describe the wall
   as open 24 hours. Neither sets separate hours for the education centre. Do not promise
   a visitor access to it at any hour.
8. **Location of the September 28 Budget Commission hearing.** WTOV9 reported the Belmont
   County Courthouse. The published notice gives the Belmont County Auditor's Office,
   101 West Main Street, St. Clairsville. Unresolved.
9. **The Budget Commission vote tally.** WTRF reports two members voted against the cuts. I
   found no official minutes. Unconfirmed.
10. **The $16 million carryover figure** for the Board of DD. Attributed to Auditor Cindi
    Henry by The Times Leader; no second source.
11. **The next Budget Commission meeting on Tuesday, October 6.** Single source, WTRF. I
    found no posted agenda.
12. **Headline attendance figure for the September 28 hearing.** The Times Leader says "more
    than 300"; WTOV9 says "hundreds." No official count exists that I could find.
